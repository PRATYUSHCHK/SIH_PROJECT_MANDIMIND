================================================================================
                        MANDIMIND — PROJECT OVERVIEW & CAPABILITIES
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MandiMind is an AI-powered agricultural demand, price, and inventory intelligence
platform designed to support decision-making for farmers, agricultural traders/sellers,
and market analysts.

Core Value Proposition:
- Enables agricultural stakeholders to make data-driven procurement and planting
  decisions before market prices shift.
- Reduces post-harvest spoilage and overstocking by quantifying risk and expected profit.
- Provides actionable recommendations with clear explainability (SHAP / feature-gain)
  rather than "black-box" predictions.
- Employs strict data-transparency standards (clearly labeling simulated demo data
  vs. AI forecasts vs. live feeds).

3. CORE SYSTEM CAPABILITIES & FEATURES
================================================================================

[A] SELLER & TRADER INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD
- AI Procurement Recommendations: Generates optimal purchase quantities (e.g., BUY 250 KG),
  expected profit in INR, confidence scores, and risk classifications (LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, CRITICAL).
- Demand & Price Forecasting: 7-day expected demand curves and price trend direction
  (with upper and lower confidence bounds).
- Inventory Health Monitoring: Tracks commodity age, spoilage risk based on weather
  (temperature & humidity), and alerts overstocked or depleted items.
- Supply & Demand Imbalance: Calculates mandi surplus or shortage risk.

[B] FARMER CROP RANKING & PLANNING
- Land & Budget Optimization: Ranks crops (Tomato, Onion, Chilli, Potato, Spinach)
  based on land size (acres), irrigation type (drip/canal/rainfed), soil type, and budget.
- Risk-Adjusted Opportunity: Evaluates expected yield, revenue, input costs, and water sensitivity
  to compute net return without promising guaranteed profit.
- Jargon-Free Explanations: Formats recommendations in accessible, farmer-friendly terms.

[C] SPATIAL & MARKET COMPARISON ANALYTICS
- Interactive Mandi Map: Visualizes regional mandis (e.g., Kothapet, Azadpur, Vashi, KR Market)
  with geospatial location markers, current arrival volumes, price trends, and weather overlays.
- Cross-Market Arbitrage & Comparison: Compares purchase price, transport cost, distance,
  and mandi quality scores to highlight optimal buying/selling mandis.

[D] ML PREDICTION & OPTIMIZATION PIPELINE
- Demand Forecasting (`demand_xgb`): Gradient boosting model predicting commodity demand.
- Price Forecasting (`price_xgb`): Forecasts expected market price and directional trend.
- Supply Forecasting (`supply_xgb`): Predicts market arrivals and oversupply/shortage probability.
- Spoilage Model: Computes decay probabilities based on perishability class, humidity, temperature,
  and stock turnover days.
- Procurement Bounded Optimization: Uses SciPy `minimize_scalar` to maximize expected net profit
  under budget, storage capacity, and spoilage constraints.
- Model Explainability (SHAP TreeExplainer): Deconstructs AI forecasts into key impacting drivers
  (e.g., rainfall impact, seasonality, recent demand, inventory age).
- Anomaly Detection: Uses IsolationForest and z-score overlays to catch price spikes and market drops.

[E] "WHAT-IF" MARKET SCENARIO SIMULATOR
- Dynamic Scenario Testing: Allows sellers to adjust weather parameters, arrival growth %,
  demand shifts, and budget limits to simulate market outcomes before committing capital.
- Delta Analysis: Computes percentage changes in expected profit, revenue, and spoilage risk.

[F] ADMINISTRATIVE & MODEL GOVERNANCE
- Model Performance Tracking: Displays benchmark evaluation metrics (MAE, RMSE, MAPE)
  comparing gradient boosting models against naive baselines.
- Real-time ML Service Health Checks: Detects whether FastAPI ML backend is reachable
  and gracefully falls back to deterministic local optimization heuristics if offline.
- System Alerts: Manages market anomaly warnings, oversupply alerts, and stock degradation notices.

4. DATA TRANSPARENCY STANDARD
================================================================================
MandiMind enforces strict data labeling across all responses:
- "SIMULATED": Pre-seeded historical demo series (clearly declared in header badges).
- "AI_FORECAST": Outputs generated by machine learning models.
- "HISTORICAL": Evaluation split metrics for model verification.
- "LIVE": Reserved exclusively for verified government feeds (disabled by default in demo mode).
